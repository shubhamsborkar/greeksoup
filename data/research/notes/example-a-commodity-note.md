An example of a note that is not about a listing. Its subject is a commodity, rubber, and its period is the quarter being researched, Q2 FY26, so it files next to every other rubber note and every other Q2 FY26 note without a folder to keep.

A sector note and a macro note work the same way: pick what the note is about, name the sector or the theme, and give it a period. The Ask box on the Commodities screen reads notes like this one. Replace it with your own.
