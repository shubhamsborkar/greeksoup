## The drawer

Funds, Capitol, Macro and Commodities carry **Your list** in the top bar. The options tape on Desk · Home carries it on its panel. Click it and a drawer opens on the right with every row the screen runs on, the ones that shipped marked *starter*.

- **+ Add a fund**, a member, a series, a commodity, a name: a short form, the fields the screen needs, Save.
- **Edit** (the pencil) on any row. Editing a starter makes it your copy, in place; the starter steps aside.
- **Remove** (the cross). A row you added is kept aside and the strip at the bottom offers **Undo**. A starter is put away.
- **Bring the starters back** returns every starter you put away, in one click.

The screen rebuilds after a change; a list that reads the public record (Funds, Capitol) takes a minute to fill.

## What each list takes

- **Funds**: the fund's name and its CIK, the number on its 13F filings. EDGAR's company search gives it from the name.
- **Members**: the full name, the surname the disclosure feed files them under, and the chamber.
- **Macro series**: any series FRED publishes, by its id (DGS10, CPIAUCSL), a label, a unit and a group. Groups become the page's sections; a group you name goes after the shipped ones.
- **Commodities**: a label, a short id, a group, a unit, a Yahoo symbol for a daily series (CL=F, HG=F, ZW=F) or a FRED series where Yahoo has none, and the industries a move lands on, cost side and revenue side, one per line.
- **The options tape**: the index or stock codes your broker serves option chains for, in the broker's own spelling.

## The index Risk measures against

Risk measures your home book against your home market's index. The top bar of Risk takes any other index symbol (^NSEI, ^GSPC, ^FTSE, ^N225) and a name for it; **Follow the market** is the way back.

## Where your rows live

In your research vault, under `lists`, one plain file per list, beside your notes and your chains. They sync, back up and restore with the rest of the vault, and a newer version of the desk brings them up to its shape after keeping a copy. The shipped starters stay beside the desk's code and never overwrite yours.

## The watchlists and the book

Watch · Home, Watch · US, Global and Desk · Book were always yours and work as before: add a name by typing it, remove it with the cross. The ⋯ that appears on any name across the desk adds it to a watchlist from wherever you are.
