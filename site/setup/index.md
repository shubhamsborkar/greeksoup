## The Settings screen

Open the desk and click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. Three tabs.

**Connect**

- **Broker.** Pick yours from the list, paste the keys it asks for, and click *Save and connect*. *Check the connection* tells you what the broker answered. For a broker with a daily login, today's token has its own box here. Below it, a **home market** choice for a reader with no broker.
- **Data provider.** Financial Modeling Prep today, or the name of a provider file your agent wrote. *Check what it allows* runs every endpoint against your plan and shows what answered. [Data providers](/docs/setup/data-providers/).
- **Your AI.** First the apps you already pay for on this computer (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI), each with *Use it for Ask* and *Sign in*; then fourteen presets for a key from a lab or a model on this computer. *Test it* sends one small request. [Your AI](/docs/setup/your-ai/).
- **Connect your AI app.** The address of the page any AI app on your computer can read, with a Copy button.

**Your desk**

- **Screens.** Which screens show in the sidebar; the rest follow your home market.
- **How you invest.** Your style, what you look at first, sectors, risk, holding period, and a few lines in your own words. Your AI reads this too, so it answers you and not a stranger.
- **Where the vault lives.** Beside the desk, or in a folder a drive you already use syncs. *Bring it back* is the way back. [Two computers](/docs/setup/two-computers/).
- **Journal.** Ask, always or never: how the journal takes the moments the desk sees.
- **This desk.** Where it runs, the *Start with the computer* switch, the *Newer versions* switch, your e-mail for the SEC, and *Show the startup guide again*.
- **Your files.** Every file in your data folder with a plain description, a *Download a backup* button, and *Restore a backup*.
- **If something is wrong.** The first things to try, and where the log is. [The full page](/docs/install/if-something-is-wrong/).

**Plugins**

- The plugins in this desk, the list we publish, and a folder or a zip from this computer. [Plugins](/docs/setup/plugins/).

## Where it all goes

One settings file inside the desk folder, written by this screen. Nothing is sent anywhere. [What it talks to](/docs/install/what-it-talks-to/).

- [Your AI](/docs/setup/your-ai/): an app you already pay for, a key from a lab, a model on your own computer, and the page your AI app reads.
