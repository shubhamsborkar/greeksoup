## What it shows

**The grid.** Symbol and exchange, last price, day move, open, high, low, previous close, volume, the fifty-two week low and high. With a broker that streams, the last price flashes green or red as it ticks and a HI or LO badge marks a name trading at the day's extreme. Where the broker serves levels, the book columns show the best bid and ask.

**Results ahead.** Under the grid, the home market's results calendar for the names on the list, where the market has one (India and the United States do).

**The session badge** at the top says whether the home market is open, and which market that is.

## Where each number comes from

| Number | Source |
|---|---|
| Quotes, levels, live ticks | Your broker, where it serves quotes for the market |
| Quotes with no broker | Yahoo's free feed, filtered to your home market's exchanges |
| Results calendar | The exchange's own calendar for markets that publish one; the free feed otherwise |
| The listing behind a name you type | The broker's security master where it has one, else Yahoo's search |

## Without a home market

With no broker and no country picked in Settings the screen says so ("no home market set") and takes plain Yahoo symbols. Pick a country under Home market on [Settings](/docs/screens/settings/) and the grid takes that market's shape. Readers whose home market is the United States see [Watch · US](/docs/screens/watch-us/) instead, and this entry is hidden by default.

## What you can do here

- Add a name at the top; the search confirms the listing before it is added.
- Filter the list; remove a name with the cross that appears on hover.
- Click a name for its [ticker page](/docs/screens/ticker/), or hover for the ⋯ menu.
- Read-only. This page cannot place orders.
