## What it shows

**Your notes on the name**, first: what you have written, the status you gave it (watchlist, researching, thesis built, invested, exited), the files attached, and the tasks due on it, with a results date already there from the Calendar.

**The chart.** The desk's own: candles, bars, Heikin-Ashi, line, area or a drawdown view; daily or weekly bars; indicators from a menu; ranges from a day to the maximum history; drawings (levels, trendlines, a note on a bar) kept as a plain file beside your notes; Save chart to notes; full screen. TradingView's embedded chart at a click, with their data and their tools, where their embed carries the listing.

**The cards.** Valuation, quality and balance sheet, earnings, news, the analyst street view (average target, implied move, the buy to sell count), the fifty-two week range, insider activity from Form 4, dividends, and about the company.

**A home-market name** opens with the exchange's own record where the market has one: quarterly results, corporate filings, shareholding, where it trades, and the session. [Markets](/docs/markets/).

**A name you hold** shows the held block: the position from the broker, Desk · Book or the US book, and the futures book where the broker reports one.

## Where each number comes from

| Card | Without a key | With a data provider key |
|---|---|---|
| Chart and quote | Yahoo's free feed | The same, with longer history |
| Profile, ratios, targets, analyst counts | Yahoo's free feed, when it is not rate-limiting | The provider |
| Insider activity | SEC EDGAR Form 4, read by the desk | The same |
| Statements, ratio history, segments, estimates, peers, dividends history, news | Not shown | The provider, six years |
| The DCF sandbox | Not shown | Seeded from the provider's statements, every input yours to change |
| Exchange filings and results on a home-market name | The exchange's own record, no key | The same |

When the free feed is rate-limiting, the page shows the last read it has, says so on an amber line with the time, and refreshes on its own when the feed answers again.

## What you can do here

- Note on the name, attach a file, add a task, set the status.
- Ask SuperAnalyst with the page open: the question carries the page's numbers and your notes, files and chart pictures on the name.
- The ⋯ on the name: watchlist, chain, note, task.
