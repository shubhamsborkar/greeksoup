## What it shows

The list grouped into Indices, FX, Commodities, Crypto and Stocks, each row with price, day percent, previous close, high, low, volume, and the fifty-two week low and high. The badge at the top reads Mixed sessions when some of the list is trading and some is closed.

The starters are the world's main indices, three currency pairs, crude and gold, and Bitcoin. Every one of them can go, and any Yahoo symbol can join.

## Where each number comes from

Yahoo's free feed, for every row. Nothing on this screen uses a key or a broker.

## What you can do here

- Add a name at the top: a company name in any market, an index symbol, a currency pair or a future in Yahoo's form.
- Filter; remove with the cross on hover; click a stock for its [ticker page](/docs/screens/ticker/).
- Read-only. This page cannot place orders.
