## What it shows

**The next ninety days**: two month grids with a dot per event, results in orange, ex-dividend and dividend paid in green, filings in grey, and held names in orange text. The header counts the events, the results among them, and how many fall on names you hold.

**Day by day**: the same events as an agenda, each with what it is, the consensus where the feed has one (EPS expected, revenue expected), and whether the name is held or watched.

**This week** and **Results ahead**: the two lists a reader looks at first, beside the agenda.

**Landed**: the filings that arrived at the SEC on your US names in the last weeks, each with its form and a link to the document.

A results date is the company's own once announced and an estimate until then, and the agenda says which. When a data provider is connected the macro prints ahead join the agenda.

## Where each number comes from

| Event | Source |
|---|---|
| Results dates, EPS and revenue expected, ex-dividend and pay dates | Yahoo's free feed |
| Filings landed | SEC EDGAR's filing index for each US name |
| Home-market results calendar | The exchange's own calendar where the market publishes one (India, the United States) |
| Macro prints ahead | The data provider key |

## What you can do here

- Nothing to add: the calendar follows the names you hold and watch across every book and list.
- Click a name for its [ticker page](/docs/screens/ticker/); a results date is already a task on [Notes](/docs/screens/notes/).
