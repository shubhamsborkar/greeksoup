## Desks

- [Desk · Home](/docs/screens/desk-home/): your broker account, priced live, and the US panels under it.
- [Desk · Book](/docs/screens/desk-book/): a portfolio kept by hand, any market, no broker.
- [Risk](/docs/screens/risk/): every book against its index; beta, drawdown, concentration, correlation.

## Watchlists

- [Watch · Home](/docs/screens/watch-home/): your home market's grid, through the broker or the free feed.
- [Watch · US](/docs/screens/watch-us/): the US grid, with the fifty and two hundred day columns on a key.
- [Global](/docs/screens/global/): any symbol from any exchange on one grid.

## Intelligence

- [Funds](/docs/screens/funds/): the 13F tracker from SEC EDGAR, with what changed since last quarter.
- [Flow](/docs/screens/flow/): the options tape on every US name, from CBOE.
- [Short](/docs/screens/short/): FINRA short interest and the daily short-volume ratio.
- [Capitol](/docs/screens/capitol/): Congress trading disclosures on your names.
- [Calendar](/docs/screens/calendar/): every dated event on every name you hold or watch.

## Market

- [Macro](/docs/screens/macro/): FRED series in groups, the home market's cards, the economic calendar.
- [Commodities](/docs/screens/commodities/): fifty-four cards and the industries each move squeezes or helps.
- [Chain](/docs/screens/chain/): your own value-chain maps, drafted by your AI, priced live.

## Research and the rest

- [Notes](/docs/screens/notes/): the research vault as one screen.
- [Any ticker](/docs/screens/ticker/): one page a name, chart to filings.
- [Settings](/docs/screens/settings/): three tabs, nothing required.

Screenshots on these pages were taken on 19 September 2026 on a copy with no data key and a paper broker account, so they show the free record as it was that day.
