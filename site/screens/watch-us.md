## What it shows

Ticker and exchange, price, day percent and change, volume, open, high, low, previous close, fifty-two week low and high. Then three columns that fill with a key: distance from the fifty day average, distance from the two hundred day average, and market cap. On a keyless copy they show a dash, as in the screenshot.

The last price flashes green or red when it ticks up or down since the last look, and a HI or LO badge marks a name trading at the day's high or low. The badge at the top says whether the US market is open.

## Where each number comes from

| Number | Source |
|---|---|
| Everything on a keyless copy | Yahoo's free feed |
| The fifty and two hundred day distance, market cap | The data provider key |
| The listing behind a name you type | Yahoo's search, US exchanges; an ISIN is looked up through OpenFIGI |

## What you can do here

- Add a name at the top by company name or ticker. A name added here is also on the [Flow](/docs/screens/flow/) tape within a minute.
- Filter the list; remove a name with the cross on hover.
- Click a name for its [ticker page](/docs/screens/ticker/), hover for the ⋯ menu.
- Read-only. This page cannot place orders.

The names on this list feed the [Calendar](/docs/screens/calendar/), [Short](/docs/screens/short/) and [Capitol](/docs/screens/capitol/) as watched names.
