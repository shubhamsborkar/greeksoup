Reading the calendar…
Where it comes from. Results dates and dividend dates from Yahoo's free summary for any exchange, with Nasdaq's own site as the second source for a US listing on the days Yahoo says too many requests; filings from the SEC's own index (EDGAR) for US listings; the home market's results calendar from its exchange where the desk has one; and the macro calendar when a data provider is connected. A results date is the company's own once it has announced it, and an estimate from past reporting dates until then; the row says which. The desk rereads all of it every six hours, and every fifteen minutes while any name could not be read, so nothing here needs a refresh from you. A results date also lands on Tasks by itself.
