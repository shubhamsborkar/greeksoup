RESEARCH DESK
Two different FINRA datasets, kept apart on purpose. Short INTEREST (left block) is the bi-monthly consolidated settlement report: actual open short positions, settled , change vs the prior settlement, days-to-cover at recent volume, and the position against FMP's float count. Short VOLUME ratio (right block) is the share of each DAY'S off-exchange volume marked short: tape flow, not open positions; ~40-50% is normal market-making, the signal is the change against a name's own average.
Reading FINRA files…
Short interest settles twice a month and publishes ~9 days later, so the settlement shown may trail today's positioning. Days-to-cover = short position / average daily volume. Daily short volume covers off-exchange (TRF) trades only; exchange-floor volume is not in the file, another reason the two numbers must not be mixed. OTC names without FINRA equity data are omitted.
