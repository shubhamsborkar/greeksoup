If something is wrong

A screen is blank or a number looks off. Reload the page once. Most screens rebuild on their own within a few minutes; the free quote feeds sometimes ask a new desk to wait, and the desk retries by itself.

The address does not open. The desk is not running. Open the desk folder and double-click Start Desk; with the switch above on, it starts by itself at your next login.

Anything else. The desk writes what it is doing to . Open the desk folder in your AI agent and say: read README.md, then read the log file and tell me what is wrong with the desk, and paste what you see on the screen. That is the same method that built it.
