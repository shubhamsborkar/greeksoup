RESEARCH DESK
Everything the desk needs from you, on one screen. Each key is optional, each one is yours, and each one stays in a file inside the desk folder on this computer; the desk writes that file for you, and a new version never touches it. Nothing on this screen places an order anywhere.

Broker

The home screens take the shape of the market your broker trades in: its session hours, its index on Risk, its currency, its exchanges, and where the public record has them, the results calendar, the filings and the market's own macro cards. With no broker connected, pick one here.
No broker, or one with no API? Desk · Book keeps a portfolio by hand in any market with no key; most broker exports paste straight into it.

Data provider

Without a provider the desk gives what the free public record offers: quotes and charts for any exchange, filings, insider trades, short interest, the macro board, the commodity board. A provider adds parsed statements, estimates, segments, peers, dividends and news on the ticker page, longer price history, and the market-wide insider and Senate scans.
Any plan works; the check says what yours answers.
The desk ships one provider's reads today, and the file that turns any other provider into the same reads is a job for your AI agent: open the desk folder in it and say write a data provider for <name> from its documentation, to the contract in data_providers/CONTRACT.md. The key and address you save here are what that file reads.

Your AI

Two shapes exist: the OpenAI chat shape, which most providers and every local model server speak, and the Anthropic messages shape, which Anthropic speaks and a few others offer as a second door. The preset picks the right one; change it only if your endpoint says so.
Optional. A key from any lab, or a model running on this computer through Ollama or LM Studio, which need no key. Once it is set, Ask · your AI in the sidebar (or ⌘I) opens a box on every screen: your question goes with that screen's own numbers to this model, and nowhere else, and the answer comes back in the box. The test sends one line to the model and shows what came back.

Connect your AI app

Any AI agent that can open addresses on this computer (Claude Code, Codex, Kimi Code, Grok Build, OpenClaw, Cursor and the like) reads the whole desk through one page. Give it this address and it knows what every screen is and where to read the numbers.
A line that works in any of them: Read and go through my desk; tell me what changed today and where the numbers came from.

Screens

Click a screen to show or hide it. The cross that appears next to a screen in the sidebar hides it too, and a hidden screen stays in the sidebar under Hidden with Show one click away. Your choices survive every update.

How you invest

Optional, and none of it leaves this computer. What you set here goes on the agent page above, so any AI that reads the desk shapes its answers to the way you invest instead of to a stranger. Click to pick; type to add your own.

Where the vault lives

Your research vault (notes, files, the journal, the tasks, the statuses, the plugins) is plain files in one folder. Point it at a folder inside a drive you already sync, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Syncthing, and the same vault is on your other computer the moment its desk points at the same folder: no service, no account, your own storage. The desk moves the vault there now; if a vault is already in that folder, the desk adopts it and folds this one in, keeping any file that differs aside under cache/previous. Open the desk on one computer at a time for the smoothest sync.

Plugins

A plugin is a folder in data/research/plugins that adds a screen to the sidebar, blocks a note can carry, or a door (a way for the Ask box to talk to something on this computer, such as the coding agent you already have). It runs on your computer and reads what you let it; what each one talks to is written beside it. One click removes it. Nothing is brought in on its own.

Journal

A name entering or leaving Desk · Book or a watch grid, a status changing, a note or a file saved, an answer kept: the desk can write each as a line in your journal, one plain file per day in data/research/journal, with room for your one-line why. Ask holds each moment until you say this time or not now; Always writes them without asking; Never writes nothing. Your own lines go in from the Journal on the Notes screen whatever you choose here.

This desk

The SEC's filing servers ask every program for a contact address and answer faster to one that gives it. Any address you own; it is sent only to the SEC.

Your files

Everything you have put into the desk is a plain file in the data folder, and the folder is yours: open it in any editor, copy it to another computer, or hand it to your agent. A new version never overwrites a file you changed. The backup below is that folder as one zip, with your keys and the downloaded market data left out.
Download a backupA restore writes the backup's files back into the desk; any file it changes is kept aside first, so it can be undone.

If something is wrong

A screen is blank or a number looks off. Reload the page once. Most screens rebuild on their own within a few minutes; the free quote feeds sometimes ask a new desk to wait, and the desk retries by itself.

The address does not open. The desk is not running. Open the desk folder and double-click Start Desk; with the switch above on, it starts by itself at your next login.

Anything else. The desk writes what it is doing to . Open the desk folder in your AI agent and say: read README.md, then read the log file and tell me what is wrong with the desk, and paste what you see on the screen. That is the same method that built it.

Reading. The README explains every screen in plain words; the technical notes in the same folder are for the agent. Newer versions arrive through the strip at the top of every screen.
