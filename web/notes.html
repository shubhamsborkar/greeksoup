RESEARCH DESK
Your research vault: the notes you write and the files you bring in, kept as plain files in the desk's own data/research folder. A note says what it is about (a listing, a commodity, a sector, the macro picture, or nothing in particular), the quarter it is researching, the project it belongs to, and links to other notes with [[double brackets]], so a company page shows every note about it, a project shows every name and note inside it, and one note leads to the next. An annual report, a model or a screenshot attaches to a note and lives in the vault with it. Open the folder in Obsidian or any editor and the same files are there; your AI agent reads and writes them too. Nothing is saved here unless you save it.
