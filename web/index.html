Connecting to desk…

Marks: the broker's own live marks where it hands them out, the prices it values the account at; a broker that gives none is marked from Yahoo's free feed.

Margin: where the broker reports a trading limit, the margin-used bar shows what is blocked against it; brokers report this differently, so cross-check the exact figures on your broker's own page.

Orders: the desk reads the account and places no orders.
