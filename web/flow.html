RESEARCH DESK
What the US options market is positioned for on each of your names, read from CBOE's free 15-minute-delayed chains (-day expiry window). The names are the US lines on Desk · Book and the names on Watch · US; a name added below goes on Watch · US and its chain is read within a minute. Unusual strikes = today's volume at least twice the existing open interest with real premium: positions that did not exist yesterday. OI builds compare open interest against the previous daily snapshot on disk, so they appear from a name's second covered day. Describes positioning only; it makes no call.
Reading chains… a full sweep takes about a minute.
PCR = put/call ratio (OI = standing positioning, VOL = today's tape). Walls = the strikes with the most open interest in the window; dealer hedging tends to pin near big walls into expiry. Expected move = ATM straddle cost on the nearest expiry, the move the market has paid for. Skew = 5% OTM put IV minus 5% OTM call IV on the first expiry past 20 days; rising skew is downside fear. Delayed public data; OI updates overnight at OCC.
